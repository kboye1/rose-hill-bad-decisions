# Version 18 — McShane, the gym and a livelier sandbox

## Restored buildings

The map extends north from Edwards Parade to restore the existing dedicated McShane Campus Center, glass arcade and Rose Hill Gym models. It retains the five central buildings, ram sculpture, fountain and courtyard. The original approximate footprint coordinates are restored from the [official illustrated campus map](https://www.fordham.edu/media/home/admin-use-only/Rose-Hill-Campus-Map.png); the map is still deliberately compressed, approximately 180 by 253 game meters. Compared with the former broad-campus movement boundary, playable ground remains about 77% smaller.

[HLW’s McShane project](https://www.hlw.com/project/fordham-university-mcshane/) was rechecked for the new glass arcade’s relationship to the older gym complex. The existing completed-exterior model retains the lower glazed wing, dark masonry piers, taller glass tower, recessed entry, canopy and connector. Its detailed photographic basis is recorded in [CAMPUS-REFERENCES.md](CAMPUS-REFERENCES.md).

The [Fordham Athletics centennial article](https://fordhamsports.com/news/2024/10/28/general-the-rose-hill-gym-celebrates-100-years) and its [entrance photograph](https://dxbhsrqyrr690.cloudfront.net/sidearm.nextgen.sites/fordham.sidearmsports.com/images/2024/10/18/1920x1080_RHG100-exterior.jpg) remain the gym references: gray fieldstone, raised gable, medallion, Tudor portal, traceried windows, wooden doors and broad two-flight steps with a landing and rails. The steps affect player and camera height.

Dimensions, hidden elevations, fine carvings and plant counts remain approximate. No source photographs or GTA assets are packaged in the game. [The version 17 research brief](EDWARDS-PARADE-RESEARCH.md) remains the reference for the central courtyard; its five-building-only crop describes that earlier version.

## Playable additions

- **Interaction wheel (Tab):** greet, roast, dance and selfie pose. G toggles dancing; T greets someone nearby. Students react and sometimes join in. Social gestures are optional, need no currency and stop when you move. Roasts add small amounts of heat and have a cooldown.
- **Ram Rally (J):** hype McShane, the gym steps and Eddie’s in order. Press E at each marker while on foot. You have 150 seconds and earn 950 chaos plus two buzz. It can be canceled or replayed. The existing three jobs and three stories are retained.
- **Character graphics:** shaped torsos and jaws, tapered limbs, facial features, layered hair, clothing seams, cuffs, belts, pockets and shoe detail. Male and female options retain the same outfits and gameplay abilities. Varied pedestrians and stationary northern hangouts make the restored spaces feel occupied.
- **Presentation:** district captions, a nearby minimap that follows the player, visible cup flights, third-person throws and a gold job-completion banner. The illustrated loading screens and saved player customization remain.

These are small-scale sandbox conventions inspired by open-world games. This remains an exterior-only browser game with stylized procedural art, not a recreation of GTA V’s production quality or complete feature set.

## Verification

Twelve automated suites cover the existing systems plus the restored map, rally stops/rewards, social interactions, paused clocks and character animation. Browser playtesting completed the rally from McShane through the gym to Eddie’s, tested male/female previews, greetings and dancing, and checked browser errors. A human playtest on the class computer remains useful.
