# Grand Theft Tuition

By **Kalie Boye**

A third-person campus comedy set around Fordham’s Edwards Parade. Explore the quad, customize your character, help friends, or take on a quick job. All missions are optional. Jobs may be timed, but free roam has no countdown.

## Play in your browser

[Open Grand Theft Tuition](https://kboye1.github.io/rose-hill-bad-decisions/) on a phone with touch controls or on a desktop with a keyboard and mouse.

## Install on a phone

- **iPhone or iPad** — Open the game link in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen** and **Add**. Turn on **Open as Web App** if Safari offers it.
- **Android** — Open the game link in Chrome. Use **Install App** on the title screen if it appears, or choose **Add to Home screen** from Chrome’s menu.

Open the game online once to download its assets. Later launches can play offline after the download finishes. Landscape gives the controls more room. Drag the left stick to move and drag the scene to look around. Tap **Use** for conversations and objectives or **Vehicle** to get in or out. In a vehicle, push the stick forward or back for gas or reverse and left or right to steer. Hold **Brake** to stop and **Boost** for a burst of speed.

On a computer, choose **Use Touch Controls** on the title screen to try this layout.

## Play locally

1. Download or clone this repository and open the project folder in VS Code.
2. In the terminal, run `python3 -m http.server 8000`. Open [localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000) in a desktop browser.
3. Wait for **Campus Ready**, click **Let’s Go**, then click **Explore Rose Hill**. **Chill** is the easier setting.

You need Python 3 and a desktop browser with WebGL. No package install is needed. If you already use the VS Code Live Server extension, you can open `index.html` with that instead.

## Rules and controls

Start at Edwards Parade. Press **B** to track a story or landmark, or **J** to choose a job. Follow the marker and use **E** to talk or act. Finish jobs to earn cash and chaos points. Your story choices change trust and campus buzz. If security starts following you, get out of sight until the stars clear.

| Keys | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **WASD**, mouse or arrow keys | Move and look |
| **Shift**, **Space** | Sprint and jump. In a vehicle, boost and brake |
| **E**, **F** | Talk or interact. Enter or exit a nearby vehicle |
| **W/S**, **A/D** in a vehicle | Accelerate or reverse. Steer |
| **B**, **J**, **P** | Contacts and journal, quick jobs, phone |
| **U**, **V**, **Tab** | Wardrobe, first/third person, social actions |
| **Q**, **X**, **Z** | Throw a cup, punch, block |
| **N**, **M**, **C**, **Esc** | Radio, sound effects, control guide, pause |

Try **Reply All** under Quick Jobs to see Duane Library and perform at Eddie’s, or **Dad’s Lambo** for a chase. The game includes adult language and fictional campus comedy.
