Grand Theft Tuition privacy policy

The iPhone app runs on your device. It does not require an account and does not send your gameplay, character alias, or settings to the developer. It does not contain ads, analytics, or tracking code.

The game stores character choices, job records, and audio preferences on your device. Deleting the app removes the local game data. The browser edition stores its own game data in your browser. You can remove it by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

The iPhone app’s gameplay works offline. If you play the website, GitHub Pages serves the game files and handles web requests under GitHub’s privacy statement.
